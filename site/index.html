A fast, minimal image viewer for Windows.

In early development. There’s nothing to download yet. Follow along on GitHub.

What it will do

  • Show the picture edge to edge. Controls appear when you move the mouse and fade when you stop.
  • Open PNG, JPEG, GIF, BMP, TIFF, ICO, WebP, SVG, PSD, DDS, TGA and Canon RAW. AVIF, HEIC and JPEG XL are planned.
  • Run as a single portable app, with an optional installer for file associations.
Free and open source under GPL-3.0.
