View image, instantly.

PicPic is a fast, minimal image viewer for Windows. Nineteen formats, basic editing, plugins and a distraction-free UI. Free and open source.

Extend it your way.

PicPic loads plugins built for Quick Picture Viewer. Drop in existing ones from the community collection, or write your own with the plugin guide.

Examples only: PicPic loads what you place in %LOCALAPPDATA%\PicPic\plugins.
